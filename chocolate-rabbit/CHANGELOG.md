# Changelog — The Chocolate Rabbit for Windows

All notable changes to the Windows app are listed here. The section for each version is shown
to users inside the app when an update is available.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-09-13

### Added
- The newsletter sign-up page: the chocolate bunny hero, The Chocolate Rabbit logo, "Join Our
  Newsletter", your name and email, and the gold SUBSCRIBE button, in Playfair Display, Allura
  and Lora.
- Sign-ups are delivered to info@thechocolaterabbit.ca through your email app (a ready-to-send
  message opens; press Send), with a local copy kept under your profile, until the newsletter
  service itself is live.
- Self-updating: the menu's "Check for updates" (and a check on launch) reads the release feed,
  shows the release notes, downloads the new setup program with a progress dialog, verifies its
  SHA-256 and installs it. You can skip any version.
- Menu: check for updates, check on launch on/off, release notes and downloads, export log,
  about, exit. F11 toggles full screen.
